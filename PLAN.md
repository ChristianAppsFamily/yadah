# Thank You Lord — Daily Gratitude Prayer Reminder App


## Features

- **Daily prayer reminders** — Local push notifications fire at up to 6 scheduled times throughout the day with the message "STOP what you are doing, take these two seconds and say THANK YOU LORD 🙏🏽"
- **Prayer moment modal** — Tapping a notification opens a beautiful full-screen overlay calling you to pause and give thanks, with an OK button to dismiss
- **Daily scripture** — A rotating scripture card shows one of 5 gratitude verses, changing each day at midnight
- **Amen button** — A large pulsing golden circle at the bottom; tap it to send 🙏🏽 emojis floating upward with haptic feedback
- **Amen counter** — Tracks today's count and all-time count, stored locally on device
- **Schedule screen** — Configure which of the 6 reminder times are active (Morning Awakening, Start of the Day, Midday Pause, Afternoon Breath, Evening Gratitude, Before Rest)
- **One-time onboarding** — First launch shows a welcome screen explaining the app and requesting notification permission

## Design

- **Background**: A serene vertical gradient — soft sky blue at top, through white, to warm cream/gold at bottom — with subtle radial light rays from the top center
- **Gold accent color** (#b07d2e) used throughout for the cross icon, headings, dividers, the Amen button border, and toggle highlights
- **Typography**: Serif design variant (New York) for headings and scripture — giving it an elegant, devotional feel. Body text in warm brown tones
- **Scripture card**: Frosted glass appearance with white translucency, soft border, and subtle shadow
- **Cross icon** at the top with a continuous soft golden halo glow animation
- **Amen button**: Large circle with pulsing concentric rings radiating outward, gold "AMEN" label, spring animation on tap
- **Floating emoji animation**: 🙏🏽 emojis rise from center and fade out like balloons when Amen is tapped
- **Dark warm brown text** on light backgrounds for excellent readability
- **No tab bar** — minimal navigation: one main screen, one settings screen accessible via a gear icon

## Screens

1. **Onboarding Screen** — Full-screen welcome with gold cross, app name, tagline ("A two-second habit that changes everything"), explanation text, and a "Turn On Reminders" button that requests notification permission. Shown only on first launch.

2. **Main Screen** — The home view showing:
   - Gold cross with halo glow at top
   - App name "Thank You Lord"
   - Today's date (e.g. "Sunday, March 30")
   - Gold divider
   - Scripture card with today's verse
   - Large pulsing Amen button at bottom
   - Settings gear icon in the top-right corner

3. **Prayer Modal** — Overlay that appears when opening from a notification:
   - Semi-transparent dark backdrop
   - Centered cream/white card with gold cross
   - "STOP what you are doing." in bold
   - "Take these two seconds and say —"
   - "THANK YOU LORD 🙏🏽" in large gold text
   - "Right now. Wherever you are." in italic
   - Gold OK button to dismiss

4. **Schedule Screen** — List of 6 reminder time slots, each with the time, moment name, and a toggle switch. Accessible from the gear icon on the main screen.

## App Icon

- A warm golden gradient background (amber to gold) with a soft radial glow at the center
- A simple white praying hands (🙏🏽 style) silhouette or a white cross symbol centered
