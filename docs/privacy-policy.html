Last updated: November 13, 2026

Christian App Empire LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the mobile application Yadah (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the App.

Information We Collect

  • Profile details you enter — First name or initials, city, and state are stored locally on your device for personalization and your Thank You Wall entries.
  • App preferences — Verse selections, reminder times, theme choice, and gratitude counts are stored locally on your device.
  • Device identifiers — Used by advertising partners (Google AdMob) to serve ads, subject to your App Tracking Transparency choice on iOS.
  • Purchase information — Processed by the Apple App Store for in-app purchases (Remove Ads Pro). We do not receive your payment card details.
  • Notification preferences — Stored locally on your device if you enable gratitude reminders.

How We Use Information

  • Display advertisements (unless you purchase Remove Ads Pro)
  • Deliver optional local gratitude reminder notifications
  • Process in-app purchases and restore purchases
  • Show your own Thank You Wall entries on this device
  • Improve app stability and user experience

Thank You Wall

At this time, Thank You Wall entries (initials and city) are stored only on your device. If a shared community wall is added later, this policy will be updated before any initials or city data are sent to a server.

Third-Party Services

The App uses:

  • Google AdMob — Advertising. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store — In-app purchases and app distribution.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, you may be asked to allow tracking for personalized ads. Ads load only after you respond to that request. You can change this choice anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

Data Retention

Local preferences remain on your device until you uninstall the App or clear app data. Ad partners retain data per their own policies.

Your Choices

  • Purchase Remove Ads Pro to disable advertisements and unlock Pro themes
  • Disable notifications in device Settings or in the App
  • Decline tracking on iOS when prompted
